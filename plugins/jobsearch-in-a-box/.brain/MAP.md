## Purpose

- `application-mailer`: Delivers the email that goes with an application and, later, the reply to an employer's message, drafted and never sent.
- `application-tracker`: Delivers the application board, every application from draft to offer with its next action and follow-up date, linked into the brain.
- `cover-letter-writer`: Delivers a short cover letter that tells the reader what the resume cannot, built only from the verified profile.
- `cv-tailor`: Delivers a resume tailored to one posting, using only facts of the verified profile, with a fact-guard report beside it.
- `job-profile`: Delivers the verified profile: every career fact the candidate can be written up from, each traceable to its source, plus the gaps.
- `job-scout`: Delivers a scored shortlist of fresh job postings that fit the verified profile, each with reasons and red flags.
- `posting-analyst`: Delivers one posting broken into role details and requirements, each marked covered, partial or gap against the verified profile.
