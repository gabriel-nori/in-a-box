# marketing-in-a-box

Role: **Marketer**. Accountable for turning what the product is into positioning and content a defined audience will understand and act on.

Skill: [`marketer`](skills/marketer/SKILL.md). Produces `positioning`.

Also: [`communicator`](skills/communicator/SKILL.md) produces `communication-guide`; [`pitcher`](skills/pitcher/SKILL.md) produces `pitch`; [`relationship-designer`](skills/relationship-designer/SKILL.md) produces `relationship-flow` (B2C and B2B).
