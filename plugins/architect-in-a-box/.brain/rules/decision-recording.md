THE RULE

Keep each significant architectural decision with its rationale, trade-offs and consequences.

What must be true at the end:
- One record per decision, kept in the project's decision log.

How it is handled:
- Capture the decision with its rationale, trade-offs and consequences. The set of records is the decision log. Use a template the team agrees (several exist).

WHY

Trigger: A decision addresses an architecturally significant requirement.

Inputs:
- The requirement, the options, the constraints.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The architect, the people affected, and the decision steward.

Expectations of the position:
- **Architect:** records the decision when it is made, not later.

RELATED

Sources (S-021): S-021 (primary, read). The page lists no fields and no timing rule; those are the team's choice.

Roles: solution-architect, decision-steward. Outcomes, not an order: the dependency edges between the roles' skills decide what runs first.
