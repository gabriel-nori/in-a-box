THE RULE

An estimate is given as a range with its assumptions and confidence, derived from the team's own history. It is a forecast, not a promise, and story points are never converted to hours.

WHY

A single number becomes a deadline the moment it is spoken, and early estimates are wide because the work is not yet understood.

HOW TO APPLY

State P50 and P85, the data they came from, and what would widen them. Send unestimable items to a timeboxed spike.

JUDGED AGAINST

No estimate in an artifact is a bare single figure.
