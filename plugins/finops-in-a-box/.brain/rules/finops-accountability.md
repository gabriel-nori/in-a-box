THE RULE

Make engineering, finance, product and leadership share accountability for cloud spend and value.

What must be true at the end:
- Allocated costs, budgets with owners, forecasts and optimisation actions.

How it is handled:
- The framework separates core personas from allied personas.
- The practitioner drives a culture of accountability and data-backed decisions and connects the others.
- Engineering makes cost-aware decisions as early in the build as possible and watches for unusual spend in the environments it controls.
- Finance and product include cloud cost in forecasting and budget planning.

WHY

Trigger: Spend is planned, changes, or is questioned.

Inputs:
- Usage and cost data, budgets, forecasts, business value measures.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The FinOps practitioner, engineering, finance, product, leadership.

Expectations of the position:
- **Each persona:** owns the cost decisions of its domain.
