THE RULE

Explain a cost change by splitting it into price, usage and commitment effects, tied to a resource, environment or deployment, and compare it with the estimate and the budget. Mark recent days as provisional because billing data lags and is revised.

- Start with the size of the change and when it started, then find the cause.
- A cause is confirmed only when the data rules out the leading alternative.
- A saving is a range with evidence, the risk of acting and a decision owner, not a to-do.
- Reading never changes resources, budgets or commitments.

WHY

'More usage' is the default explanation and is often wrong: a rate change or an expired discount looks the same on the total. Acting on a wrong cause wastes effort or breaks something in use.

HOW TO APPLY

Pull the cost report for the period, group by service and owner, compare with the estimate, split the largest changes into price, usage and commitment, line them up with deployments, and list findings with confidence and the next check.

EXCEPTIONS

A quick answer to an executive may lead with the headline and carry the evidence in an attachment.

JUDGED AGAINST

Every material change is split by driver, every figure has a source, period and lag, and savings carry a range and a risk.
