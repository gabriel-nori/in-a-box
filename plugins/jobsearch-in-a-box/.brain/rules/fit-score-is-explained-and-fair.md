THE RULE

A fit score is a number with reasons: 0 to 100 in four bands (85+ excellent, 70 to 84 good, 50 to 69 possible, below 50 poor), one to four reasons that each point at a requirement and a profile fact, and up to three red flags.

- Seniority is judged both ways. A role far above the candidate and a role well below are mismatches, capped below 70.
- Years of experience are computed from dates with overlaps counted once, by code, not estimated.
- Protected characteristics (age, gender, nationality, ethnicity, religion, disability, family status) never count for or against a posting.
- A posting too thin to judge says so and scores low.

WHY

Trigger: someone asks which vacancies are worth the effort, or ranks postings.

An unexplained score cannot be checked, and a model adds date ranges badly, so seniority judged on its estimate drifts. Reasons make the score contestable; the arithmetic in code keeps it stable.

HOW TO APPLY

Compute years with `scripts/jobkit.py years`, score, name the band with `scripts/jobkit.py band`, and show reasons and flags beside the number. Scale effort to the score: only good or better postings earn a tailored draft.

EXCEPTIONS

The candidate may deliberately target a lower or higher level; then the score notes the stretch and does not cap it.
