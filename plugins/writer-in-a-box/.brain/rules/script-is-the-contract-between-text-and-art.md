THE RULE

For a post with text and images, the text is handed to the designer as a structured script, not as loose prose.

- Each slide has a layout name, structured fields and one idea.
- Every field fits the character limit of its layout.
- The designer reproduces the script word for word; any cut is listed and sent back to the writer.

WHY

Trigger: someone asks for a post or carousel that has both text and art.

When text and art are produced by two roles, loose prose forces the designer to rewrite, and the art and the words drift apart.

HOW TO APPLY

`carousel-writer` produces `post-script`, `post-designer` maps its slides one to one into the art-spec.
