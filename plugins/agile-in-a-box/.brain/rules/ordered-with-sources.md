THE RULE

A backlog order shows its technique, every input with a source or an 'assumed' tag, the dependency overrides and the cut line. The product owner decides; the role recommends.

WHY

An order without its arithmetic is an opinion, and the loudest stakeholder wins. A visible calculation can be challenged and improved.

HOW TO APPLY

Pull value, window and ROI from the `business-case`, size from the `estimate`, fit from the `refined-requirement`; name the inputs that would change the order most.

JUDGED AGAINST

Every ranked item can be traced to inputs, and every override is explained.
