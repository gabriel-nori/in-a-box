THE RULE

A risk is accepted only by a named person with the authority to accept it at that level, on a stated date and with a review date. Scores follow one stated scale, residual risk drops below inherent only where a control is named, and risks are written as cause, event and consequence.

- Treatment is avoid, reduce, transfer or accept; "monitor" alone is not a treatment.
- Appetite is set by leadership. An assistant may propose wording but never sets it.
- Where there is no data for likelihood, say so instead of inventing a number.

WHY

A register without owners and acceptances records worry, not governance. Scores lowered without a control make the organisation look safer than it is.

HOW TO APPLY

For each risk record the owner, the scoring evidence, the treatment with actions and dates, and for acceptances the accepting person, date and review.

EXCEPTIONS

None.

JUDGED AGAINST

Every risk has an owner and a treatment, and every acceptance names who accepted and until when.
