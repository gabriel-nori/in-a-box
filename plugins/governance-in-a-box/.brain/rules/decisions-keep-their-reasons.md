THE RULE

Every decision that matters is recorded with its context, the options considered, the choice, the reasons, the decider and a review trigger. Each decision area has exactly one accountable party. A decision is superseded by a new record, never rewritten or backdated.

- RACI uses role names, and a row nobody agreed to is marked proposed.
- Approval routes scale with stakes: money, risk score, data class, reversibility.
- Emergency changes may be approved fast and are always reviewed afterwards.

WHY

When a decision cannot be explained, it gets relitigated or blindly repeated. A trail that was edited after the fact is worse than none.

HOW TO APPLY

Write the record at the time of the decision. When it changes, add a new record that links to and supersedes the old one.

EXCEPTIONS

Trivial, reversible choices inside one person's authority need no record.

JUDGED AGAINST

Every decision record names options, reasons and a decider, and every area has one accountable party.
