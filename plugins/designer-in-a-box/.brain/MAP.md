## Purpose

- `art-renderer`: Delivers the finished PNG files for an art-spec and a manifest of them, produced by a deterministic script.
- `design-foundations`: Delivers the design rules every piece inherits: composition, balance, colour, typography and accessibility thresholds, stated as checkable limits.
- `design-reviewer`: Delivers a design review of a rendered piece against its art-spec and the design rules, with a verdict and findings tied to each slide.
- `image-generator`: Delivers generated images for the slots of an art-spec, requested from a diffusion model with the designer's full brief, and the provenance of each.
- `post-designer`: Delivers an art-spec: the complete specification of a social piece, slide by slide, with layouts, tokens and any image to be generated, ready to render.
