THE RULE

Every policy, standard and exception has a named owner, a stated source (a risk, an obligation or a decision) and a review or expiry date. A policy whose source is gone is retired, and an exception never lasts without an end date.

- Policy says what and why; standard says what is measurable; procedure says how. They are kept apart.
- Each requirement is written so compliance can be observed.
- An exception carries a compensating control, an approver other than the requester and an expiry.

WHY

Policies without owners rot, and rules nobody can test cannot be audited. Open-ended exceptions turn into the real policy.

HOW TO APPLY

For each policy record owner, approver, scope, source, version and next review. For each exception record the requester, approver, reason, compensating control and expiry.

EXCEPTIONS

A draft policy may lack an approver if it is marked draft and not distributed as binding.

JUDGED AGAINST

No policy lacks an owner, a source or a review date, and no exception lacks an expiry.
