## Purpose

- `incident-commander`: Runs an incident to a restored service with one clear commander, separated roles, a live state document and a hand-off that cannot be misread.
- `observability-engineer`: Delivers an observability plan, and the instrumentation that carries it, for the tools being built and the stack they deploy on: logs, metrics, traces, APM, deployment monitoring, alerts and the rules that govern them.
- `observability-reader`: Delivers a reading of the signals a system is emitting (logs, metrics, traces, APM views, alerts, deploy markers) as findings: what is wrong or healthy, since when, how sure, and what to check next.
