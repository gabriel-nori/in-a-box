THE RULE

This plugin drafts; it never applies, sends, schedules or accepts. Every move that reaches an employer, a board, a mail service or a calendar needs the candidate's yes for that exact move, that time.

- Searches carry role keywords, place and country only; no name, history or contact detail.
- Resumes, letters and mail are handed over as text or files for the candidate to send.
- A suggested interview time is offered only if the candidate confirmed it free.

WHY

Trigger: a flow reaches the point of submitting an application, sending mail or booking an interview.

An application sent in the candidate's name is irreversible and personal. An employer reads it as the candidate's own word, and a wrong one cannot be recalled.

HOW TO APPLY

Stop at the draft. Show what would go out and to whom. Proceed only after an explicit yes naming the move. Sign any outward write `implemented by jobsearch-in-a-box - <skill>`.

EXCEPTIONS

A durable authorisation the candidate gave for that exact action (for example, send the follow-up on day seven to this recruiter).

RELATED

Skills: job-scout, application-mailer, application-tracker. The owner's rule that nothing personal goes out without explicit authorisation. Idea source: the "never sends or applies" stance of AienTech/dossier (AGPL-3.0, ideas only).
