## Purpose

- `continuity-standards`: Maps a disaster recovery or continuity setup to ISO 22301, ISO/IEC 27031, ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A and the other frameworks that apply, and delivers the clause-by-clause status and gaps.
- `dr-planner`: Delivers a disaster recovery plan: business impact analysis, RPO and RTO per service, recovery strategy, backup and replication design, and failover and failback runbooks.
- `dr-tester`: Designs recovery exercises (tabletop, restore verification, failover, full-site) with success criteria, and reads their results to report achieved RTO and RPO against target with the gaps found.
