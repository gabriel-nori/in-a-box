THE RULE

Every cost figure carries the source it came from (pricing API, calculator, price page, or the arithmetic for self-hosted), the date it was read, and the assumptions behind the quantity. The result is a low, expected and high range, with the assumption that moves it most.

- Prices are read from the provider's current source, never from memory.
- On-demand and commitment prices are shown separately.
- Data transfer, NAT and load balancing, requests, telemetry, support and licences are included or named as excluded.
- Comparisons size every option for the same workload and basis.

WHY

A bare total gets quoted, copied into a plan and defended as if it were a measurement. Prices change, and the quantity assumptions are usually where the error lives, so the estimate has to show both.

HOW TO APPLY

Build line items from the pricing API or calculator; record the date; list assumptions as given, derived or guessed; compute the range by moving the dominant assumptions; add the commitment scenario as a separate column; list what is excluded.

EXCEPTIONS

A back-of-the-envelope order of magnitude may be given in chat when labelled as such and not stored as an estimate.

JUDGED AGAINST

Each line has a source and date, the total is a range, and exclusions are named.
