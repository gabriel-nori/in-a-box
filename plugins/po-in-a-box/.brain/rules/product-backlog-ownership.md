THE RULE

Maximise the value of the product by keeping one person accountable for the Product Goal and for a backlog that is clear, ordered and transparent.

What must be true at the end:
- A Product Goal that is communicated, and a Product Backlog whose items are clear and ordered.
- Decisions the organisation respects.

How it is handled:
- The Product Owner develops and communicates the Product Goal, creates clear items and orders them, and keeps the backlog transparent.
- Stakeholders change the backlog by persuading the Product Owner, not by instructing the team.
- The order of the backlog and the reasons for it are visible, so a challenge can be answered with the inputs, not with authority.

WHY

Trigger: Continuously; most visibly before Sprint Planning and after each Sprint Review.

Inputs:
- The Product Goal and the product's market and users.
- Stakeholder requests and feedback.
- The Developers' sizes and the team's capacity.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The Product Owner (one person, not a committee), the Developers, and the stakeholders.

Expectations of the position:
- The Product Owner is accountable even when work is delegated.
- Scrum.org frames the role through stances; one is the visionary, who sets the strategic vision and intermediate goals. The full list was not read.
