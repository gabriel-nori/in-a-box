THE RULE

A statement that the plan meets a standard (ISO 22301, ISO/IEC 27031, ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A, NIST SP 800-34, DORA, SOC 2 availability and so on) names the clause or control, the evidence that satisfies it, and its status. Alignment is not certification, and the wording says which one is meant.

- Verify the current edition and clause numbers from the source; do not quote them from memory.
- "Aligned with" and "certified against" are different claims; only an accredited audit supports the second.
- Record the scope of the management system: what is in and what is out.

WHY

Compliance statements travel into contracts and audits. A vague or wrong clause reference becomes a misrepresentation.

HOW TO APPLY

Produce a table of clause, requirement in one line, evidence, status (met, partial, gap, not applicable), owner.

EXCEPTIONS

None.

JUDGED AGAINST

Every standards claim has a clause, evidence and status, and the edition is dated.
