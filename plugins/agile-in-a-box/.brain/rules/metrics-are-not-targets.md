THE RULE

Velocity, throughput, cycle time and similar metrics are used to understand and forecast the team's own flow. They are never set as targets, compared between teams or used to rate people.

WHY

When a measure becomes a target it stops measuring: points inflate, items get split to look fast, and trust drops.

HOW TO APPLY

Show trends and percentiles to the team that owns the data. Refuse requests to rank people or teams by these numbers and offer a system-level view instead.

JUDGED AGAINST

No output ranks individuals or compares points across teams.
