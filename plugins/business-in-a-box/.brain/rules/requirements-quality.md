THE RULE

Make sure requirements can be understood, built and verified by someone who was not in the room.

What must be true at the end:
- Requirements that are individually clear and, as a set, consistent and complete.

How it is handled:
- Check each requirement: unambiguous (one interpretation), verifiable (its fulfilment can be proven or measured), necessary (removing it leaves a deficiency), single.
- Check the set: no conflicts between requirements or with higher-level ones; nothing needed is missing.
- BABOK organises business analysis into six knowledge areas: planning and monitoring, elicitation and collaboration, requirements life-cycle management, strategy analysis, requirements analysis and design definition, solution evaluation.
- For stories, apply INVEST.

WHY

Trigger: Requirements are written, changed or about to be estimated or built.

Inputs:
- The requirements, their sources and the stakeholders.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The business analyst, the Product Owner, developers and testers.

Expectations of the position:
- **Business analyst:** clarifies before the build, not after.
