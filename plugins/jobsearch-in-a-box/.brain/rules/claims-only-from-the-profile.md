THE RULE

Every statement in a resume, letter or email traces to a fact in the verified profile or to something the candidate said for that application. A keyword the posting wants and the profile lacks is a gap: it is reported, never written as a skill, and never "optimised in" for an applicant tracking system.

- Tailoring is choosing, ordering, shortening and rephrasing true facts. It is never adding.
- In-progress credentials stay in progress. Numbers appear only when a number exists.
- The fact guard (`scripts/jobkit.py guard`) runs on the final text; a flag is resolved or shown, never silenced.

WHY

Trigger: someone asks to tailor, polish or optimise a CV or letter for a vacancy.

Asking a model to highlight results and fit the system pushes it to invent. A resume that claims a tool, a title or a certificate the candidate lacks fails in the first technical question, and the damage outlasts the application.

HOW TO APPLY

Recall the profile first. Match each requirement as covered, partial or gap. Write only from covered and partial. Run the guard on the draft against the profile and the posting. Give the candidate the gap list beside the draft.

EXCEPTIONS

None. A candidate who wants a claim the profile lacks adds the fact to the profile first, and it then has a source.
