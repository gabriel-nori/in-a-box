THE RULE

Review a contract against what the company can accept, and escalate what it cannot.

What must be true at the end:
- Findings per clause against the playbook, redlines, and escalations.

How it is handled:
- Separate two tools: a checklist says what to check, a playbook says what the company can agree to.
- A playbook per clause holds the standard position, the fallback, the unacceptable terms and the escalation contact, with the reason, to help negotiation.
- Law-department playbooks and review policies are part of a contract-lifecycle practice.

WHY

Trigger: A contract arrives for review, or is being drafted.

Inputs:
- The contract, the company's playbook, the commercial context.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: Legal counsel, the business owner, and the escalation contact.

Expectations of the position:
- **Legal counsel:** advises on risk and does not decide the business trade-off; escalates what the playbook does not cover.
