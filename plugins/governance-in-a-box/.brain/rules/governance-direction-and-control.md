THE RULE

Keep the decisions about direction with those accountable, and make management carry them out, with evidence.

What must be true at the end:
- Decisions on record, policies in force, risks with owners, and control findings.

How it is handled:
- Governance determines who decides; management is responsible for directing and implementing those decisions.
- ISO/IEC 38500 principles: responsibility, strategy, acquisition, performance, conformance, human behaviour; applied through evaluate, direct and monitor.
- NIST CSF 2.0 is a framework for managing cybersecurity risk; its Govern function was not read.

WHY

Trigger: A policy, risk, control or decision needs an owner, or a review is due.

Inputs:
- Objectives, risks, policies, controls and prior decisions.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The governing body or its delegate, management, and the auditors.

Expectations of the position:
- **Decision steward:** keeps the log of who decided what and why.

RELATED

Sources (S-042, S-028): S-042 (primary, search summary only). S-028 (primary, hub page only). Confidence is moderate; read the COBIT 2019 and CSF 2.0 documents before binding this process.
