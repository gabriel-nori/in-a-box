THE RULE

Prove that the recovery arrangements work, and turn what is found into improvements.

What must be true at the end:
- An exercise report with results against the targets and improvement actions.

How it is handled:
- Exercises are consistent with the scope and objectives, use suitable scenarios, validate the arrangements, produce a formal report, run at planned intervals and lead to actions.
- Vary scenarios, teams and capabilities across the programme; repeating the same tabletop is not enough. Formats include walkthrough, tabletop, simulation, component and full tests.
- Test restores from backup, not only that backups exist. For active/active, test loss of a Region and data-disaster recovery too.
- A data disaster always has a recovery time above zero and a recovery point before the disaster was found.

WHY

Trigger: On a planned interval, after a material change, or after an incident.

Inputs:
- The DR plan, the recovery targets, a scenario.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The DR tester, the teams that would execute recovery, and the plan owner.

Expectations of the position:
- **DR tester:** runs the exercise and reports honestly against the targets.
