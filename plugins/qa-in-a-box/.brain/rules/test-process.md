THE RULE

Plan, design and execute tests that give evidence about quality, and report what is known.

What must be true at the end:
- A test plan, test conditions and cases, executed results, defect reports and a completion report.

How it is handled:
- The ISTQB test process groups the work into planning, monitoring and control, analysis, design, implementation, execution and completion.
- Work products: test plan, test conditions, test cases, test procedures, defect reports, progress and completion reports.
- Compare actual progress with the plan, check against coverage criteria and decide whether more testing is needed.

WHY

Trigger: A change is ready for testing, or a test approach is needed.

Inputs:
- Requirements, acceptance criteria, the change under test.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The tester, developers, the Product Owner.

Expectations of the position:
- **Tester:** reports evidence and its limits.
