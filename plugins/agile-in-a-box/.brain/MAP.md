## Purpose

- `agile-facilitator`: Delivers a facilitated agile ceremony that ends with decisions, owners and a next step, not a status recital.
- `agile-metrics`: Delivers the charts and flow metrics that show how work is moving, with how to read each and what it does not prove.
- `backlog-prioritizer`: Delivers an ordered backlog where each ranking is explained by value, cost of delay, size and risk, worked out with the product owner and the business analyst.
- `estimator`: Delivers a size and a time estimate for work, as a range with its assumptions and confidence, never a single number.
- `facilitate-daily`: Delivers a daily that coordinates the next day of work from the board, with blockers owned.
- `facilitate-planning`: Delivers a sprint plan with a goal, a capacity-based forecast range and ready, ordered scope.
- `facilitate-refinement`: Delivers refined backlog items that meet the Definition of Ready, with splits and open questions owned.
- `facilitate-retro`: Delivers a retrospective that ends with at most three owned improvement actions, framed by mechanism.
- `issue-writer`: Delivers a tracker issue (story, bug, spike or task) that meets INVEST and the Definition of Ready, written so the builder does not need to ask.
- `working-agreements`: Delivers the team's Definition of Done, Definition of Ready and working agreements as short, checkable lists the team actually holds.
