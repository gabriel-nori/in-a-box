THE RULE

Keep the top of the Product Backlog clear enough that items can be selected in a Sprint Planning.

What must be true at the end:
- Items that are clear, sized by the Developers and ordered by the Product Owner, so the next Sprint can pick from them.

How it is handled:
- Items are broken into smaller, precise pieces and given the detail the Developers need.
- A readiness check on each story uses INVEST: independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, testable. A story that cannot be estimated needs more conversation or splitting.
- The Developers size; the Product Owner orders.

WHY

Trigger: Continuously, as an ongoing activity; teams usually hold a regular session.

Inputs:
- The Product Backlog and the Product Goal.
- Items that are not yet clear, too large or untestable.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The Product Owner and the Developers, with others invited for expertise.

Expectations of the position:
- **Product Owner:** accountable for clear items and their order; may delegate the work but stays accountable.
- **Developers:** responsible for sizing and for saying when an item is not clear enough.

RELATED

Sources (S-001, S-035): S-001 (primary) for accountabilities and sizing. S-035 (secondary, search summary) for INVEST; it is a heuristic, not a rule of Scrum. The Guide's own text on refinement was not part of the extract read; the 'ongoing activity' description should be confirmed against it.

Roles: facilitate-refinement, issue-writer, estimator, backlog-prioritizer. Outcomes, not an order: the dependency edges between the roles' skills decide what runs first.
