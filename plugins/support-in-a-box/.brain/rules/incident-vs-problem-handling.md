THE RULE

Restore the customer's service quickly through an incident, and remove recurring causes through problem management.

What must be true at the end:
- A restored service or a workaround; for recurring causes a recorded problem, a known error and a permanent fix.

How it is handled:
- ITIL 4 defines an incident as an unplanned interruption, resolved first by a workaround or quick fix; a problem is the cause, or potential cause, of one or more incidents.
- The service desk is the first point of contact for reporting incidents.
- Problem management records and investigates the underlying fault, publishes a known error when needed and drives a permanent fix; proactive work analyses incident trends.
- Knowledge is kept so responses get faster and recurrence drops.

WHY

Trigger: A customer reports a fault (incident), or the same incidents repeat (problem).

Inputs:
- The ticket, the service state, known errors and knowledge articles.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The service desk, the incident manager, and the problem owner.

Expectations of the position:
- **Support agent:** restores first, records enough for the problem owner to find the cause.
