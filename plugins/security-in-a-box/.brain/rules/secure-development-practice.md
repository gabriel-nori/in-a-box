THE RULE

Make the software secure along its life: prepared organisation, protected software, well-secured releases, and a response to vulnerabilities.

What must be true at the end:
- Findings against the risk categories, required fixes, and a record of residual risk.

How it is handled:
- NIST SSDF groups practices into Prepare the Organization, Protect the Software, Produce Well-Secured Software and Respond to Vulnerabilities.
- Review against the OWASP Top Ten 2025 categories: broken access control, security misconfiguration, software supply chain failures, cryptographic failures, injection, insecure design, authentication failures, software or data integrity failures, logging and alerting failures, mishandling of exceptional conditions.
- Residual vulnerabilities are identified, responded to, and their causes addressed.

WHY

Trigger: A change is designed, built or released, or a vulnerability is reported.

Inputs:
- The change, its design, its dependencies, known vulnerabilities.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The security reviewer, developers, and whoever owns the release.

Expectations of the position:
- **Security reviewer:** reports risk with evidence and says what would reduce it; does not approve silently.
