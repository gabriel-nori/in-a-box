## Purpose

- `controls-auditor`: Read-only auditor that maps controls to frameworks (ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO 31000, SOC 2, LGPD, GDPR, CIS and others in scope), including data governance and AI governance, collects the evidence for each, and reports what is met, partial, missing or untested.
- `decision-steward`: Keeps the record of who decides what: the RACI, the decision log with its ADRs, the approvals each change needs and the change-advisory process.
- `policy-author`: Writes and maintains policies, standards and exceptions so that every rule has an owner, a scope, a review date and a way to be waived with an expiry.
- `risk-register`: Identifies, scores and tracks risks against a stated appetite, with a treatment and an owner for each, following ISO 31000.
