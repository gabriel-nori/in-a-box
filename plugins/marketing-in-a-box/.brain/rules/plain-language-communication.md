THE RULE

Communication is written for a named audience, in plain words, and tested for understanding.

- Name the audience and what they must do or understand after reading.
- Write clearly for that audience; keep the structure visible so the point is found fast.
- Test it: if a reader from the audience cannot restate the message, it is not done.
- Tone and vocabulary are decided per audience and channel, and recorded in the guide so others stay consistent.

WHY

Trigger: someone asks for a communication guide, tone of voice, or how to write to a given audience.

The digital.gov guide groups plain language into audience, writing, design and testing. The page describes topics, not detailed rules, so the specifics are the skill's own.

HOW TO APPLY

Audience first, then message, then tone and examples of do and do not, then how it will be tested.
