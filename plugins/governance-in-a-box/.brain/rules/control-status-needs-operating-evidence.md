THE RULE

A control is marked met only with evidence that it operated over the period in scope, not with a description of intent. Design effectiveness and operating effectiveness are reported separately, and an audit never modifies the thing it audits.

- Data governance and AI governance are assessed as controls with their own findings: inventory, ownership, lawful basis or permitted use, retention, oversight, monitoring and vendor review.
- Edition and clause numbers are read from the current source and dated, not quoted from memory.
- Alignment is not certification or attestation, and the wording says which is meant.
- Personal data is never copied into findings; use counts, redacted samples and references.

WHY

Compliance claims travel into contracts, filings and customer questionnaires. A control counted on paper alone fails the first real audit, and an audit that changes systems destroys its own evidence.

HOW TO APPLY

Produce a table of control, framework references, evidence, design status, operating status and owner, plus a cross-framework map so one control is evidenced once.

EXCEPTIONS

A control not yet in operation is reported as design-only, never as met.

JUDGED AGAINST

Every status has a framework reference and evidence of operation, and the edition is dated.
