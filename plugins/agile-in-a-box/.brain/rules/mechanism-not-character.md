THE RULE

When a ceremony or a metric shows something went wrong, record what in the system allowed it (a missing check, an unclear ready item, an overloaded queue), never what it says about an individual.

WHY

People who are blamed stop reporting problems. Mechanisms can be fixed; character cannot.

HOW TO APPLY

Rewrite 'X forgot the review' as 'the review step was not part of the pipeline'. Charts and notes are by team and item, not by person.

JUDGED AGAINST

No retro note or chart names an individual as the cause of a miss.
