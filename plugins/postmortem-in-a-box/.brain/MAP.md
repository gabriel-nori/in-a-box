## Purpose

- `postmortem-author`: Writes a blameless postmortem for anything that went wrong enough to learn from (an outage, a bug that reached users, a vulnerability, an attack) and ends it with follow-up actions that have owners, dates and a place to be tracked.
- `postmortem-defect`: Writes the postmortem of a defect that reached users or production, explaining why the change process let it through and what would have caught it earlier.
- `postmortem-security`: Writes the postmortem of a vulnerability or an attack: how the weakness existed, how it was found or exploited, what was exposed and what changes, with sensitive detail handled for the audience.
