## Purpose

- `budget-planner`: Delivers a budget plan sized from the scope and complexity of the work, with build and run split, contingency, and the budgets, thresholds and alerts to configure in the provider tools.

- `cost-monitor`: Delivers a reading of actual cloud and infrastructure spend against the estimate and the budget: where the money went, what changed, what is anomalous, and what could be saved, with evidence.
