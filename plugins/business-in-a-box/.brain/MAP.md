## Purpose

- `business-analyst`: Delivers establishing whether a thing is worth doing: value, cost, risk and the numbers behind them.
- `canvas-designer`: Delivers a Business Model Canvas that shows how an idea creates, delivers and captures value.
- `process-modeler`: Delivers a BPMN process model, as-is and to-be, with a Mermaid diagram of each.
- `swot-analyst`: Delivers a SWOT of an idea, project or company, with internal strengths and weaknesses kept apart from external opportunities and threats.
