THE RULE

No relationship message goes out without a legal basis recorded and an opt-out that works, for B2C and B2B alike.

- Under LGPD, personal data needs a legal basis (art. 7). Consent must be free, informed and unambiguous (art. 5 XII), tied to specific purposes; generic authorisations are void (art. 8 §4). It can be revoked at any time, free of charge and easily (art. 8 §5, art. 18).
- Legitimate interest (art. 7 IX, art. 10) allows only the data strictly necessary and requires transparency. It is not a free pass for B2B: a business contact is still a person.
- CAN-SPAM (US) applies to B2B email too: truthful headers and subject, ad disclosure, a postal address, an opt-out honoured within 10 business days, and responsibility stays with the sender when a vendor sends.
- Each flow states its basis, its opt-out step, and what happens to the contact after opt-out.
- This is design guidance, not legal advice; send the flow to a lawyer before launch.

WHY

Trigger: someone designs an email, WhatsApp, nurture or follow-up flow, or a customer relationship journey.

LGPD was read up to the first 100,000 characters; the rest was not. ANPD guidance on legitimate interest returned 404 and was not read, so the legitimate-interest test (necessity, balancing, safeguards) is only what art. 10 says.

HOW TO APPLY

Separate B2C and B2B flows. For each, write basis, first touch, cadence, opt-out and consent record, and exit.
