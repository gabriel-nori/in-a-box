THE RULE

Say who the product is for, why it is different, and get it to market with sales and customers ready.

What must be true at the end:
- Positioning, messages, a go-to-market plan and enablement material.

How it is handled:
- Positioning differentiates the product from competitors in a crowded market.
- Translate technical concepts into messages that state the value.
- Plan the launch across channels and track results.
- Gather and synthesise market and customer data into messaging.

WHY

Trigger: A product or feature is about to launch, or its position is unclear.

Inputs:
- Product facts, market and customer insight, competitors.

HOW TO APPLY

Participants: The marketer, the Product Owner, sales and customer-facing teams.

Expectations of the position:
- **Marketer:** the voice of the market inside the company.
